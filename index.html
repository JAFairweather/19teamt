What is 19TET?

19-tone equal temperament divides the octave into 19 equally spaced steps of ~63.16 cents each, instead of the 12 steps used in standard Western tuning.

The result is strikingly pure major and minor thirds, a rich palette of 7 additional notes per octave, and a system that has fascinated musicians and theorists since 1577.
